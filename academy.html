PROGRAMME CONTENT

What the Academy covers

Practical content across six areas designed to prepare graduates and early-career professionals for the workplace.

01🏢

Workplace Readiness

Professional conduct, workplace culture, communication and the practical skills that help new professionals thrive from day one in any organisation.

02📄

CV, LinkedIn & Application Skills

How to write a credible CV, optimise your LinkedIn profile and approach job applications with a clear, targeted, ATS-aware strategy.

03🎤

Interview Preparation

Understanding interview formats, preparing structured answers, managing nerves and communicating your experience with clarity and confidence.

04💼

Employability & Career Skills

Foundational skills employers value — adaptability, professional communication, job search planning and personal effectiveness in the workplace.
