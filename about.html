01 — OUR STORY
"Employers need capable people whose skills and experience match the demands of a role. Professionals need clear career direction, credible professional profiles and a sound understanding of how recruitment decisions are made."
MJ Global Career Strategy — Founding Principle

Two audiences.
One integrated brand.

MJ Global Career Strategy is a Kenya-based career and HR solutions brand supporting individuals as they build meaningful careers and helping organisations strengthen their people practices.

Its work brings together recruitment and talent acquisition, practical HR support, career coaching, professional branding and career development resources — combining HR knowledge, recruitment experience, career strategy and the considered use of digital tools.

🏢 For OrganisationsRecruitment, HR support and people process solutions
👤 For IndividualsCareer coaching, CVs and professional development
02 — DIRECTION

Vision, Mission &
Strategic Direction

Vision

To become a trusted career and human resource solutions partner recognised for strengthening organisations and supporting sustainable career growth.

Mission

To connect people and opportunities through reliable recruitment support, practical HR solutions and career development services that help individuals progress and organisations build capable teams.

Strategic Direction

MJ Global aims to grow from a career services brand into a broader HR and career solutions practice — strengthening recruitment, developing HR consultancy, expanding career programmes, building the Graduate Academy and serving clients across Kenya and other markets. Growth will be guided by service quality, responsible practices and the ability to deliver on agreed commitments.

PROFESSIONAL FOUNDATION
04 — THE FOUNDER

Grounded in HR.
Focused on people.

MJ Global Career Strategy was founded by a human resource management professional and certified career coach with a Bachelor of Science in Human Resource Management from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT).

The founder's professional work spans HR support, recruitment and career services — including recruitment coordination, candidate sourcing and screening, onboarding support, HR documentation, employment process administration and career document development. This combination informs the brand's dual focus on employer needs and the candidate experience.

The founder takes an active interest in the use of technology and AI in HR and career services, with an emphasis on responsible use, data privacy and human judgement.
